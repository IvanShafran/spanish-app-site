Privacy Policy — SpanishApp for Android

Last updated: November 2026.

SpanishApp has no account, no analytics and no advertising. On Android, when the app crashes, or hits an error it handled, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so the developer can fix the bug: the crash log (for a handled error, a stack trace and the name of the part of the app that failed, never the error message), your device model, the Android version and a random Firebase installation ID. Nothing else is sent, and none of your words or progress. You can turn crash reports off in Settings → Privacy settings.

The words you browse, any words or tags you add yourself, and your app preferences are stored in local storage on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. On Android, the system also backs up your word lists, practice progress, read articles and settings to your own Google account through Android Auto Backup, and restores them when you reinstall or move to a new phone. We never receive this backup. You can turn it off in Android's system backup settings. You can also save your words, tags, read articles and practice progress to a backup file in a location you choose, and restore it later. The app does not send this file to the developer.

Email the developer at support@shafran.dev. The support email includes app and device details that you can edit or remove before sending.
